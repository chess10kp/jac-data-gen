We're running a hackathon and I'm writing the judging backend as a Jac service (`app.jac`). Teams and judges can already be registered via the two `def:pub` functions. I need three `walker:pub` endpoints (called as `POST /walker/<name>`; the body fills the walker's `has` fields; the client reads `data.reports[0]`). Everything is stored in the graph under `root` and must persist between requests.

1. **`score_team`** — body `{"judge": <name>, "team": <name>, "criterion": <"IMPACT"|"CRAFT"|"DEMO">, "points": <int>}`. Validate in this order and report `{"ok": false, "reason": ...}` on failure: unknown judge → `"unknown judge"`, unknown team → `"unknown team"`, criterion not one of the `Criterion` enum names → `"bad criterion"`, points outside 1..10 → `"bad points"`. Otherwise store the score as a `Scored` edge from the judge to the team and report `{"ok": true}`. If that judge already scored that team on that criterion, **replace** the old points instead of adding a second edge.

2. **`leaderboard`** — body `{"track": <str>}` (empty string = all tracks). Visit the teams (only that track, if given). A team's score is the **average per judge**: sum of all its points divided by the number of distinct judges who scored it, rounded to 2 decimals (0.0 if nobody scored it). Report one list of `{"team": name, "score": score, "judges": number_of_judges}` sorted by score descending, then team name ascending.

3. **`judge_progress`** — body `{"judge": <name>}`. Report the sorted list of team names this judge has **not yet scored on all three criteria**. Unknown judge → empty list.
